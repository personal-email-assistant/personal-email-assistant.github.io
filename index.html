Personal Email Assistant

A private, single-user email assistant for its developer's own mailboxes.

Personal Email Assistant helps its developer keep up with email across the developer's configured personal, nonprofit, and community mailboxes, which the developer is authorized to access. It is not a public service: it runs on the developer's own computer and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Reads mail from the developer's Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook.com, and Microsoft 365 mailboxes and provides it to the developer's AI assistant for briefings about requests, commitments, deadlines, and follow-ups. Every result identifies the mailbox it came from. Briefings can also run on a schedule the developer sets up.
  • Tracks follow-ups in a small local list that stores follow-up metadata such as status, classification, deadlines, and hashed message references, not message content.
  • Prepares drafts and organizes mail. It can save or update drafts; change read status; archive messages or restore them to the Inbox; apply or remove Gmail labels; move Microsoft messages between permitted folders; and create two organizing labels or folders, "Assistant - Read Later" and "Assistant - Records". Every change is first previewed, then needs a separate confirmation tied to that exact change, which the developer gives through the AI assistant.

What it does not do

  • It provides no tools to send, reply to, or forward email, or to move email to Trash or delete it.
  • It provides no tools to download or upload attachments.
  • It does not change mailbox settings, rules, forwarding, or delegates.
  • It does not sell or rent email data, or use it for advertising. Email content and metadata are shared with the developer's AI service to provide these features, as the privacy policy explains.

See the privacy policy for exactly what data the app accesses and how it is handled, and the terms of service.
